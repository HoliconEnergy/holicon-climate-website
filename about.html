The partnership behind Holicon Climate

Better together.
Built around you.

Two established local businesses have brought their specialist skills together to make heating and cooling simpler, better connected and easier to support.

The right expertise, working as one.

A great heating or cooling installation depends on more than the equipment itself. Heating design, pipework, electrical supply, controls and the way a building uses energy all need to work together.

Holicon Climate combines M Yould & Sons’ established plumbing and heating experience with Holicon Energy’s electrical and renewable-energy knowledge. Each brings specialist capabilities; together they can coordinate the details that matter to your project.

For you, that means fewer gaps between trades, better communication from the outset and a dedicated team to help you from your initial enquiry through installation and aftercare.

The right trades, working together

For you: fewer gaps between trades.
02

Decisions based on the whole property

We can consider how a heat pump or air conditioning system fits with your building, existing services and wider energy setup — including solar PV and battery storage where relevant.

For you: a solution suited to the bigger picture.
03

One coordinated experience

Rather than leaving you to bring different specialists together, our aim is to make planning, installation and communication clearer through a single dedicated Climate team.

For you: less organising, more clarity.
04

Experience you can build on

Holicon Climate is a new dedicated brand, but the people and practical knowledge behind it come from two established businesses with experience across domestic and commercial work.

For you: confidence in the team behind the work.
05

Support beyond handover

Getting comfortable with a new system matters as much as fitting it. We are building clear homeowner guidance, customer support and routes to request servicing or help when something isn't right.

For you: an easier path to ongoing support.
