Cooling & heating

Not just for summer.

Can air conditioning heat my home?

Reversible air-to-air systems provide both heating and cooling, but do not normally supply domestic hot water.

Can I have it in multiple rooms?

Single and multi-split options can serve different rooms. Outdoor unit capacity and pipe routes need assessment.

How noisy is it?

Noise varies by model, operating mode and location. We consider both indoor comfort and neighbours.

Where can it be installed?

Bedrooms, living rooms, offices and garden rooms are common applications. Wall, floor and concealed units may be suitable.

Does it need servicing?

Follow manufacturer filter cleaning guidance and arrange appropriate servicing.

How much does installation cost?

Unit choice, pipe routes, electrical supply and access affect the price.
