Frequently asked questions

Can air conditioning heat my home?

Many modern air-to-air systems provide both heating and cooling. Suitability depends on the property and system design.

Does an air source heat pump work in winter?

Yes. A correctly designed system can provide heating in cold weather, although efficiency and output vary with conditions.

Can you service a system installed by another company?

You can submit a service enquiry. We'll review the system details and confirm whether we can assist.
