Heat pump FAQs

Will an air source heat pump work in winter?

Yes. Correctly designed systems can provide heating in cold conditions. Output and efficiency depend on the outdoor temperature, the property and the system design.

Should I leave my heat pump running all day?

Many heat pumps work efficiently with steady, relatively low flow temperatures. The best schedule depends on your home's insulation, heat emitters and controls; avoid making major setting changes without understanding your system.

Can a heat pump provide hot water?

Many air-to-water heat pumps can heat a suitable domestic hot water cylinder as well as provide space heating.

Air conditioning FAQs

Can air conditioning also heat a room?

Many modern air-to-air heat pump systems provide both heating and cooling. We'll advise on suitability when assessing a property.

How often should I clean the filters?

Check your manufacturer's instructions. In many systems, reusable indoor filters need regular inspection and cleaning, particularly during heavy use.

Can you service a system fitted by someone else?

You can make an enquiry. We'll review the manufacturer, model, location and work required before confirming whether we can assist.
